/synctowebhook - Sync Stock ke Webhook

Kamu bisa lakukan opsi:

Opsi 1 - Shortcut Format:
/synctowebhook "article_code": ABC123, "site_code": SS20, "company_code": COMPANY1, "stock": 100

Opsi 2 - Label Format:
article_code: ABC123
site_code: SS20
company_code: COMPANY1
stock: 100
timestamps: 2026-09-02T23:05:00Z (Opsional)

Jika timestamps tidak diisi, akan otomatis gunakan waktu saat ini.
